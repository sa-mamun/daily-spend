DAILY SPEND

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026 · Applies to Daily Spend for Android
Package: com.samamaun.expensetrackerlite

This policy explains how Daily Spend handles information when you use the app.

Daily Spend records and organizes expenses on your device. No account is required. The app does not send your expense records to a developer-operated server and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter: expense amounts, dates, optional notes, categories and subcategories. It also stores app preferences such as language, appearance and onboarding status. These records are stored in a local SQLite database in the app's storage.

This information is used to display your expense history, calculate totals and charts, produce monthly and yearly reports, and remember your preferences. Daily Spend does not connect to your bank or request bank login credentials.

Collection and sharing

The app does not transmit your expense records or app preferences to us. We do not sell those records or share them with advertisers. The current app does not include advertising, third-party analytics or a developer-operated cloud synchronization service.

Daily Spend does not use your contacts, camera, microphone or location for expense tracking. Expense entry and reports work offline.

Android backup and device transfer

Android backup is enabled in the app. Depending on your device, Android version and backup settings, the operating system may back up app data to your configured backup provider or transfer it to another device. These backups are managed by Android and your device or backup provider, not by Daily Spend. We do not have access to those backup copies.

You can manage backup and restore through your device settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its local data may not remove copies previously held by your backup provider.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain in local app storage until you delete them or remove the app's data. You can edit or delete individual expenses. Deleting a category or subcategory can also delete its associated expenses after confirmation.

Use Settings → Clear all data to remove expense records and custom categories and subcategories. The app recreates its default categories; appearance, language and other preferences may remain. To remove all local app data, including preferences, use Android's app storage settings to clear storage, or uninstall the app. Separately manage any operating-system backups as described above.

We cannot retrieve or delete local records remotely because we do not hold a server copy.

Security

Records are stored in the app's device storage and rely on Android's application isolation and your device's security controls. The app does not add a separate encrypted database or app passcode. Protect your device with a screen lock and avoid storing sensitive credentials in expense notes.

Children's privacy

Daily Spend does not ask users to provide their age or create an account, and the app does not transmit their expense records to us. If you have a concern about a child's use of the app, please contact us.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process standard technical information, including your IP address, to operate and secure its hosting service. This is separate from the expense records stored inside the app. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page has no advertising, analytics scripts, forms or tracking cookies added by us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published at this address with a revised effective date.

Contact
